About the company

Dream Team Trading P.L.C. was established in 2019 as an office supplies vendor, printing and advertising company, and later expanded into the export sector. Currently engaged in manufacturing, the company is also constructing a bottled spring water factory. Its core mission is to deliver customer-centric services.

Ethio Build Connect Expo integrates the construction, real estate, building materials, finance, insurance, contracting, architecture and consulting sectors within a single business platform. It showcases 3D construction printing, prefabrication, smart-city solutions and green, energy-efficient technologies, to expand foreign direct investment, accelerate technology transfer and create jobs.

Ethio Build Connect ExpoProposal · 05 / 12

Vision, objectives and strategic design

In its long- and medium-term plans, the Government of Ethiopia has set rapid urban growth, a better-organized housing supply, wider use of modern construction technologies, stronger private-sector participation and an expanded financial system as key goals. This expo supports those goals by bringing the real estate, construction and finance sectors together on one integrated platform.

Main objective

To strengthen networking and working relationships by bringing the real estate, construction and finance sectors together on one platform.

Specific objectives

1
Participating organizations present their projects, services and products to the public
2
Winning new customers and clients
3
Growing sales of locally made products
4
Introducing finance options (loans, insurance, microfinance) directly to visitors
5
Showcasing new construction technologies and materials

Vision

To be the preferred business and digital platform for Ethiopia’s real estate and related sectors, making an outstanding contribution to sustainable trade.

Mission

Streamline trade through a transparent, well-coordinated exhibition; showcase locally made products; provide digital platforms; strengthen trust between buyers and sellers; and take part in events in regional cities and abroad.

Core values

Transparency · Integrity · Innovation. These values guide the service we give every participant, visitor and partner.

This integrated, multi-sector approach is designed to close the trust gap between buyers and sellers, grow sales of locally made products, and give Ethiopia’s construction sector a digital platform that lasts beyond the five exhibition days.

Market study

Urban growth in Ethiopia is rapid. In Addis Ababa in particular, high migration into the city has created strong demand for housing and a serious shortage. Ethiopians and people of Ethiopian origin living abroad are increasingly keen to invest at home, and banks and financial institutions are building mortgage schemes. Yet the lack of organized, transparent information that buyers can easily reach erodes the trust that should exist between buyers and sellers.

Exhibition layout

70–90 standard booths (3×3 m each) are arranged around a shared VIP stage, a coffee lounge and question-and-answer zones. All are built to the same design, safety and accessibility standards, with one consistent brand look shared with the entrance gate and the main stage. The coffee lounge serves traditional Ethiopian coffee to encourage networking.

Entrance and walkways

LED light boxes at the main entrance make it visible from a distance and inviting. The entrance keeps visitors moving and safe; banners showing Ethiopia’s real estate growth line both sides of the walkways.

Entrance gate design

A 4 m high, 6 m wide entrance arch with a geometric design and integrated LED display panels, built from durable, lightweight, weather-resistant materials. Visitors scan a QR code to download the app or open the website.
